Backup & privacy

Keep control of your wardrobe data.
Local-first means local.
This app has no account, analytics, advertising or cloud database. Your entries and photos stay in this browser on this device. This is not encrypted storage: use your iPhone passcode and keep your device secure.

Export creates a JSON file containing your item details and photos. Save it to Files, an external drive, or iCloud Drive if you choose. Importing a backup adds its contents to this device.

Important: Safari and iPhone storage can be cleared by the user or managed by iOS. Export backups regularly. iCloud device backup behavior depends on your iPhone settings; this app does not independently sync its database to iCloud.
